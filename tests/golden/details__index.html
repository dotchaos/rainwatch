About this forecast

Method
  • Intensity tiers: Light <0.10"/hr · Moderate 0.10–0.20 · Heavy 0.20–0.50 · Very heavy >0.50.
  • Radar dBZ → rate with Marshall-Palmer (Z=200R1.6): 30 dBZ ≈ 0.11"/hr, 35 ≈ 0.22", 40 ≈ 0.45". Capped at 55 dBZ.
  • Next ~2 h: the latest radar frame is moved along the measured storm motion (cross-checked against the 700 hPa steering wind). The chance of a heavy hour is the share of 735 variations that deliver ≥0.20": 49 nearby upstream positions × arrival 20 min early to 20 min late × rain rate 25% lower to 25% higher. Capped at 95% (85% when motion confidence is low). Needs ≥2 frames.
  • Later hours: share of pooled ensemble members (ECMWF, GFS, ICON) at or above the threshold. Members are pooled, never averaged with other sources. 3-hourly ensembles smooth short bursts, so compare with HRRR/NBM in the Basis column.
  • NWS gridded QPF arrives in 6-hour blocks and is shown spread evenly; it is a cross-check, not a timing source.
  • Nothing is invented: a missing source is listed under Problems and its column shows —.
