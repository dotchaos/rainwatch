## 1. Radar escalation safeguard (`rainwatch/escalation.py`, wired in `engine.build`)

For radar-primary hours only (0–2 h), the radar's **drains-falling-behind** and **driveway** shares stand only with
independent corroboration near that hour (t−1..t+1):
- model: pooled ensemble at that level in >= 10% of runs, or any single run (HRRR, ECMWF…) flagging that level;
- gauge: the Mint gauge's latest completed hour (still reporting) >= 0.20";
- official: an NWS flood product (watch, warning, advisory) in force.

Without it, the level falls back to the ensemble's own share for that hour (or 0). Unchanged: radar amounts,
timing, p_heavy (Rain boots and the heavy-rain push), the 0.20" "feel" level, every model hour, thresholds and
weighting. NWS alerts keep their own push path and never pass through the gate. Raw radar shares and the evidence
are kept on the row (`levels.radar_raw`, `levels.corroborated`, `levels.gated`) and archived (`lv`, `lv.radar_raw`).

## 2. Lead-time scorecards (`rainwatch/scorecard.py` → `data/leadtime.json`, `data/leadtime.md`)

1–3 h and 3–6 h, per source, vs SFOC1 only: MAE, bias, heavy hits / misses / false alarms (POD, FAR, CSI),
calibration for heavy and measurable rain (observed frequency only where a bin has >= 10 hours). One verdict per
gauge hour per bucket (latest issue in the bucket). The card's own words and gear are archived once per clock hour
(`data/cards/YYYY-MM.jsonl`: headline, detail, gear line, upcoming, gear rung per graph hour), built by
`card/src/lib/cardtext.ts` (same derivations as the card) in a new workflow step, and scored hour by hour against
the gear the gauge supports. Runs every update; never blocks it. Tests: `tests/test_scorecard.py` (5),
`card/src/lib/cardtext.test.ts` (2).

## Addendum (John's review, Nov 16): approved with two additions

1. **0–1 h scorecard** (the hour in progress and the next; leads in (−1, 1) h), same Mint-gauge verification. The
   primary forecast is also split by what carried each hour: radar-only, radar-corroborated (with the corroborating
   sources counted), or models, each with its own MAE, bias and heavy hits / misses / false alarms. Hours archived
   before this change carry no corroboration list; for them it's inferred from the archived sources at that hour
   only, and the scorecard says how many. 1–3 h and 3–6 h scoring is unchanged (checked: identical output, old vs
   new code, on the live archive). On the archive at merge time, 0–1 h radar-only: 71 hours, 1 miss (Sun 2 PM),
   2 false alarms (one is Sun midnight); radar-corroborated: 3 hours, 1 false alarm.
2. **Radar-only heavy push** reads "Heavy rain possible in the next hour" (or "possible <hour>" beyond it) and says
   no model, gauge or NWS product shows it yet. It is still sent. Corroborated (ensemble >= 10% heavy, a model run
   >= 0.20", the Mint gauge's last hour >= 0.20", or an NWS flood product): the existing probability wording. NWS
   alerts keep their own words and priority. The heavy-rain probability is unchanged.

Diagnostics kept per radar hour: `radar_heavy.corroborated`, `levels.corroborated`, `levels.radar_raw`,
`levels.gated`; archived as `src: radar` and `rc: {heavy, behind, driveway}`. Corroboration thresholds are
provisional (one model run counts); the record of who corroborated is there to judge whether that was meaningful.
